Our Story

A Decade of Building Exceptional Shopify Stores

Certified Shopify Partner 10+ Years Experience 230+ Projects

Founded in 2015, Medito Digital was built with one clear mission: to help brands build Shopify stores that don't just look great — they perform. Over the past decade, we have grown into a full-service Shopify agency trusted by brands across 4 continents.

As a certified and trusted Shopify Partner, we combine deep technical expertise with creative design thinking to deliver stores that convert visitors into loyal customers. From startups to enterprise brands, we have helped over 230 businesses scale faster on Shopify.

Our team of expert designers, developers, and growth strategists work as an extension of your team — transparent, dedicated, and always focused on results that matter to your business.
